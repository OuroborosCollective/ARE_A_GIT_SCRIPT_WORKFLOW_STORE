# ARE Store Design System

## Visual direction

ARE uses a dark engineering-lab aesthetic: deep graphite surfaces, acid-lime signal accents, warm amber evidence markers, precise borders, and compact engineering metadata.

The visual metaphor is an instrument panel for trustworthy automation:

- Signal = portable candidate
- Warning = adaptation required
- Evidence = source revision + blob SHA + manifest
- Consent = explicit human control over publication and marketing

## Icon

The ARE mark is a high-contrast orbital A/revision glyph. It is designed to stay recognizable at launcher and repository-icon sizes.
